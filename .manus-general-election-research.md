## Implementation cautions

- Treat the campaign’s actual spend, field plan, polling, and vendor pricing as user-supplied or verified campaign data. Do not infer them from press coverage.
- Present any lower-spend plan as a scenario with assumptions, pacing, measurement checkpoints, and tradeoffs—not a win forecast.
- Update the dashboard’s primary-election language, election countdown, opponent research, media strategy, and geographic framing for the Nov. 3 general election.

## Browser verification

Both Nashville Banner and Tennessee Lookout were read in the browser on Aug. 15, 2026. They independently confirm that Hatcher won the Republican primary, will face Columbia Mayor Chaz Molder in November, and is campaigning in a substantially redrawn district. Tennessee Lookout reports Hatcher’s 53.2%–46.8% primary result and Trump’s post-primary general-election endorsement. Nashville Banner reports Molder’s pre-primary fundraising exceeded $2.5 million and notes that later FEC filings should be used before treating the figure as a current resource estimate.

## Planning scenario — not a forecast

The dashboard may model a **$240,000 / 80-day lean paid-media scenario** ($3,000 average pacing per day) as an alternative to a larger agency proposal whose budget and scope have not been supplied. Suggested allocation: $72,000 CTV/streaming video, $60,000 online video, $36,000 Meta, $36,000 programmatic display/retargeting, $12,000 search, and $24,000 CRM/SMS/email/list activation. The model must state that it does not include production, field, mail, research, legal/compliance, or agency-management costs unless explicitly supplied, and that the campaign should compare it against the agency scope before acting.
