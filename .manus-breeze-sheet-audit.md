# Breeze Sheet Audit — Aug. 16, 2026

## Approved source tabs

Only the following Google Sheet tabs are eligible for the Breeze portal import:

| Tab | Allowed use | Observed status |
|---|---|---|
| `✅ VALID (Send These)` | General approved outreach cohort | 5,222 data rows returned, all marked `valid` in the ZeroBounce status field. |
| `⭐ VALID GOLD (Best)` | High-priority approved outreach cohort | 438 data rows returned, all marked `valid` in the ZeroBounce status field. |

All other tabs—including invalid, abuse, spam-trap, unsubscribe, unknown, and rejected views—are excluded by product requirement and must never be ingested into portal KPIs, lead journeys, email activation lists, or individual lead views.

## Safe mapping fields

The valid-gold tab exposes the following usable field categories: first and last name, city/state/ZIP, email, ZeroBounce status and substatus, email account/domain/provider metadata, last-known-activity metadata, and merge status. The portal will only retain fields essential to the selected operational view and will never expose full contact details in a public route.

## Mapping caveat

The valid tab’s exported first data row appears without a header row, while the valid-gold tab exports headers. The importer must apply the reviewed valid-gold column schema to valid-tab positional fields or require a header row before importing it automatically. This must be resolved before production synchronization.
